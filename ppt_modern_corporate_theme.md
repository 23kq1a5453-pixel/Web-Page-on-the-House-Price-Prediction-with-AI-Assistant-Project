# Modern Corporate Theme for Final Year Project Presentation

## Slide 1 — Title
House Price Prediction Using Machine Learning
Final Year Project

Design style:
- Dark navy background
- White or light gray text
- Blue/teal accent colors
- Clean modern typography
- Minimal icons or simple house symbol

## Slide 2 — Introduction
- Real estate pricing depends on multiple factors.
- Market value is often hard to estimate manually.
- A data-driven approach improves decision-making.

Design style:
- Professional corporate color palette
- Simple geometric shapes
- Clean left-aligned content

## Slide 3 — Problem Statement
- Prices vary by area, location, age, and features.
- Traditional valuation methods are slow and inconsistent.
- There is a need for an intelligent prediction system.

Design style:
- Strong headline text
- Minimal clutter
- Use one visual icon or chart illustration

## Slide 4 — Objectives
- Predict house prices using machine learning.
- Analyze key housing attributes.
- Improve valuation accuracy.
- Develop a user-friendly application.

Design style:
- Four small cards with icons
- Blue accent highlights
- Crisp spacing

## Slide 5 — Scope and Significance
- Residential property price prediction.
- Useful for buyers, sellers, and investors.
- Demonstrates ML application in real estate.

Design style:
- Smart layout with short bullets
- Light corporate branding
- Business-focused tone

## Slide 6 — Methodology
- Collect and prepare housing data.
- Select relevant features.
- Split dataset into train/test sets.
- Train model using Random Forest Regressor.
- Evaluate performance with metrics.

Design style:
- Flowchart style diagram
- Blue and teal step markers
- Clear process flow

## Slide 7 — Algorithm Used
- Random Forest Regressor
- Handles tabular data efficiently.
- Works well with non-linear patterns.
- Produces stable and accurate predictions.

Design style:
- One main heading with supporting text
- Simple diagram or tree icon
- Minimal but modern look

## Slide 8 — Results and Evaluation
- R² Score: model fitness measure
- MAE: average prediction error
- RMSE: overall prediction deviation
- Results indicate strong predictive performance

Design style:
- Metric cards with accent colors
- Professional dashboard style
- Keep numbers prominent

## Slide 9 — Demo / Output
- User enters property details.
- Model predicts estimated price.
- Output is displayed in a clear dashboard.

Design style:
- Screenshot of app UI
- Highlight key input fields and prediction result
- Clean presentation with white cards

## Slide 10 — Conclusion and Future Work
- Project successfully predicts house prices.
- Useful for real-estate decision support.
- Future scope: real datasets, better models, and deployment.

Design style:
- Strong closing statement
- Nice gradient or corporate blue highlight
- Minimal final callout section

## Corporate Presentation Tone
Use a confident academic-business tone with:
- professional language,
- concise bullet points,
- clean spacing,
- dark blue and white theme,
- strong metric highlights,
- and a polished final-year showcase feel.
